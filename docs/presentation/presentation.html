Engineering Discipline

The Descope Ladder: What We Cut & Why

✕
Cut: Flipkart & Meesho Parsers

Rationale: We prioritized 100% deep compliance with the Amazon V2 24-column settlement specification rather than writing shallow, fragile parsers across 4 marketplaces.

✕
Cut: Volumetric Weight Guessing

Rationale: Guessing package dimensions without warehouse 3D-scan photos causes rejected disputes and penalizes seller account health. We strictly audited weight handling against declared catalog weight slabs.

✕
Cut: Fuzzy Order Matching

Rationale: Fuzzy string matching in financial reconciliation produces disastrous false joins. We maintained exact order-ID joins and isolated bank UTR reconciliation into an independent D6 reporting leg.
